Meet Catherine Flowers

Founder of Triangle Fingerprinting

Welcome to Triangle Fingerprinting, where decades of law enforcement experience meets state-of-the-art fingerprinting technology. Catherine Flowers, founder of Triangle Fingerprinting, brings a unique combination of Army Military Police veteran service and 25 years working with attorneys in the legal community to serve the Raleigh-Durham-Chapel Hill Triangle area's fingerprinting and mobile notary needs.

As a U.S. Army Military Police veteran and retired law enforcement officer, Catherine has spent over two decades since retirement building trusted relationships with law firms and attorneys throughout North Carolina. Her extensive background working in the legal community ensures that every fingerprint capture meets the highest standards of quality and compliance.

State-of-the-Art Technology: Live Scan & Traditional Ink Fingerprinting

Triangle Fingerprinting operates both Live Scan electronic fingerprinting equipment and provides traditional ink fingerprinting services to meet all client needs. Catherine's commitment to using the best technology ensures optimal acceptance rates for:

  • NC SBI Electronic Submissions - Direct transmission to NCSBI at 3320 Garner Road, Raleigh per state protocols
  • FBI Background Checks - Channeler services with guaranteed acceptance
  • Out-of-State Licensing - FDLE (Florida), Georgia, Texas, California requirements
  • Professional Licensing Boards - NC Medical Board, NC Board of Nursing, NC Real Estate Commission
